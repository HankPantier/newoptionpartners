Who we are

High-touch brokerage, without the layers.

NewOption Partners is an independent commercial real estate firm serving Boulder County and the northwest Denver metro. We are small on purpose. Every assignment gets senior-level attention from start to finish — no junior handoff, no account team you never meet.

Our clients run from startups signing their first lease to Fortune 500 tenants and institutional investors. We put maximum effort and detailed attention into each project, and build deal terms around what actually serves your organization — financial structure, length of occupancy, specific building requirements — on your timeline. We work alongside you to locate, negotiate, and acquire the right property for your operation. When you put your faith in us, we commit to total transparency, clear communications, and a professional, organized process.

What we do

Four disciplines. Elegant, common-sense solutions.

Office and industrial tenant and buyer representation, lease and sale marketing, investment sales, and land sales and development consulting. We bring the whole of our vast experience to bear on each facet of every deal.

01

Tenant & Buyer Representation

Space searches, site selection, and negotiation for office, industrial, warehouse, and flex users. We run the process so you can keep running your business.

02

Leasing & Sale Marketing

Positioning and marketing for owners who want their office or industrial space in front of the right tenants and buyers — not just posted to a listing service.

03

Investment Sales

Owner-user and investment sales of office and industrial property, from a single building to a multi-building portfolio, with the underwriting to back the pricing.

Land & Development

The team

Who you'll actually be working with.

Paul Whiteside

Managing Broker

Forty years in commercial real estate, built on major tenant representation assignments — Intrado (300,000 SF), Bright Horizons, Kahuna Ventures, Ski Magazine, PopSockets — and significant office, medical office, and portfolio sales, including Boulder's 19-building Flatiron Business Park.

Since the pandemic he has focused on urban infill land in Boulder and Denver, including the sales underlying the redevelopment of Williams Village and Basemar Shopping Center.

Over the years Paul has built strong relationships with tenants, landlords, landowners, national developers including Landmark Properties and Core Spaces, lenders, and investment banking professionals across the country. Combined with deep local market knowledge, those relationships let him deliver results on either side of a transaction.

He has served on several local boards, primarily in animal welfare — including the Boulder Valley Humane Society and Greenwood Wildlife Rehabilitation. He holds a BS from the University of Colorado Boulder. Go Buffs!
