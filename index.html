Who we are

High-touch brokerage, without the layers.

NewOption Partners is an independent commercial real estate firm serving Boulder County and the northwest Denver metro. We are small on purpose. Every assignment gets senior-level attention from start to finish — no junior handoff, no account team you never meet.

Our clients run from startups signing their first lease to Fortune 500 tenants and institutional investors. We put real time into each project and build deal terms around what your organization actually needs, on your timeline.

The team

Who you'll actually be working with.

Paul Whiteside

Managing Broker

Forty years in commercial real estate, built on major tenant representation assignments — Intrado (300,000 SF), Bright Horizons, Kahuna Ventures, Ski Magazine, PopSockets — and significant office and portfolio sales, including Boulder's 19-building Flatiron Business Park.

Since the pandemic he has focused on urban infill land in Boulder and Denver, including the sales underlying the redevelopment of Williams Village and Basemar Shopping Center.

Over the years Paul has built strong relationships with tenants, landlords, landowners, national developers including Landmark Properties and Core Spaces, lenders, and investment banking professionals across the country. Combined with deep local market knowledge, those relationships let him deliver results on either side of a transaction.

He has served on several local boards, primarily in animal welfare — including the Boulder Valley Humane Society and Greenwood Wildlife Rehabilitation. He holds a BS from the University of Colorado Boulder. Go Buffs.

Aaron Evans

Broker Associate

A commercial broker with NewOption since 2002, Aaron has advised everyone from tech startups to established law firms on office space, and industrial users on warehouse requirements.

He is also an investor and asset manager for several office and multifamily properties — first-hand experience that lets him negotiate while genuinely understanding both sides' needs and psychology.

His work spans tenant representation for leasing, owner-user buyers and sellers, and marketing investment and owner-occupied buildings for sale. The goal on every assignment is the same: an informed, targeted search that carries straight through negotiation and acquisition.

Aaron holds a bachelor's degree in Biochemistry from the University of Colorado.
